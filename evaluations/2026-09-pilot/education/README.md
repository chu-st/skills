# Provenance of the education case

Unlike the [support case](../support/draft.md), which uses synthetic data, this
case is real. The [frozen draft](draft.md) is a verbatim two-sentence excerpt from
an unpublished internal working document of the author's own product research, and
it is published here deliberately, with the author's consent, as the material under
review. It mentions that product's interface in the first person.

The excerpt was chosen because its two defects are authentic: they were found by a
review of that internal work, not planted for this pilot. Invented defects tend to
be either too obvious or unrealistic, so a draft with documented real errors makes
a better test of whether a reviewer finds what it should find.

Nothing here identifies a client, a person, or any third party's data. The two
predefined defects are listed in [rubric.json](../rubric.json); the reviewers'
answers quote the excerpt back while discussing it.
