Two source summaries, supplied corpus only.
Harvard institutional report, 2015, https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2015/04/massive-study-on-moocs/ : the full study covers 68 courses. The comparison of 59% versus 5% certification covers 12 courses offering an ID-verified certificate. Participation in the paid track was chosen, not randomized.
Nielsen, https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/ : more than two disclosure levels usually create usability problems. Additional levels are possible; when complexity cannot be reduced, group advanced features and perform extra usability testing. This is general interface guidance, not a measurement of the training interface mentioned in the draft.
